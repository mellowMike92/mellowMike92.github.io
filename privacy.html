Privacy Policy

GridAndLedger Pin Scheduler — a personal-use application operated by a single individual (the "Operator"). Effective date: October 24, 2026.

What this app is

This app is a scheduling tool used solely by its Operator to publish Pins to the Operator's own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the Operator.

Information accessed

With the Operator's explicit OAuth authorization, the app accesses the Operator's own Pinterest account to create boards and Pins. The app does not access, collect, or store any other Pinterest user's data.

Information stored

The app stores only: (a) OAuth credentials for the Operator's own account, held on the Operator's own infrastructure, and (b) the Pinterest IDs of boards and Pins the app created, used solely to avoid duplicating its own posts. No analytics, tracking, or personal data of any end user is collected or stored.

Sharing

No information is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. API credentials are kept private and are never transmitted anywhere except to Pinterest's own OAuth/token endpoints.

Data retention & deletion

Stored board and Pin IDs may be deleted at any time by deleting the app's state file. Revoking the app's access in Pinterest account settings immediately ends all access.
