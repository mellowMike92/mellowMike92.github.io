GridAndLedger Pin Scheduler

A personal-use scheduling tool for a single Pinterest business account, operated by the owner of GridAndLedgerStudio (an Etsy shop selling spreadsheet templates).

What it does

The app publishes one Pin per day to the operator's own Pinterest business account. Each Pin is original marketing content the operator wrote and designed for products sold in the operator's Etsy shop, and links to that product's Etsy listing. Every Pin is created and reviewed by the operator before it is scheduled. The app does not automate engagement of any kind (no following, saving, commenting, or messaging) and does not act on behalf of other Pinterest users.

Data use

The app accesses only its operator's account via OAuth 2.0, after the operator's explicit authorization. It stores only the IDs of Pins and boards it created — needed to avoid re-posting — and collects no end-user data. See the Privacy Policy.

Contact

Operated by the owner of GridAndLedgerStudio on Etsy. Contact via Etsy shop messages.
